System Architecture Roadmap

An overview of our software evolution, operating environments, and upcoming physical hardware deployments.

01
Phase I • Legacy Foundation Active & Maintained

Galaxia Platform

Our initial platform release. Galaxia established our foundational web architecture, integrated tools, and multi-user networking capabilities. Due to continued demand, Galaxia remains actively updated and maintained.

02
Phase II • Browser OS Primary Production

Comet OS

A major architectural advancement. Comet OS introduced a single-file web operating environment equipped with window management, desktop utilities, proxy handling, and system-level applications.

03
Phase III • Next Generation In Development

Polaris Ecosystem

The primary focus of our current development cycle. Polaris expands our software capabilities to interface directly with physical hardware, microcontrollers, and real-world system deployments.
